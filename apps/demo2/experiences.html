THE RULE

One meaningful thing per day.

Why it works

When every hour is booked, a place becomes scenery. One anchor gives the day a shape without removing the chance to wander.

How to choose

Pick something you cannot do at home. A meal, a trail, a conversation, a workshop or a ritual is usually enough.

Build a trip around one great day.

Start with a destination, then choose one experience worth protecting on the calendar.
