Practical notes

Travel smarter, not harder.

Useful frameworks for deciding where to go, what to carry, how much time to leave open and what is actually worth paying for.

PLANNING

The 3-anchor method

Choose one neighbourhood, one experience and one meal worth planning. Everything else can stay flexible.

BUDGET

Spend where it changes the day

Put money into location, sleep and one memorable experience. Save on everything that does not change how the trip feels.

TRANSPORT

When to take the slower train

If the route itself is beautiful, travel time is not lost time. Build the journey into the itinerary.

PACKING

The smaller-bag checklist.

Pack for the first three days. Laundry solves the rest.

Always pack

Passport + digital copy
Universal adapter
One light layer
Comfortable walking shoes
Reusable water bottle
Small first-aid kit

Think twice

Extra shoes
Full-size toiletries
“Just in case” outfits
Heavy books
Bulky camera gear
Anything you can buy locally
